# Reward Issuance Policy & Utility Economics

Policy Version: `policy-2026.1`

---

## 1. The Policy Equation

$$\text{VTIME Issued} = \min( \text{base reward} \times \text{evidence multiplier} \times \text{policy multiplier}, \text{category cap}, \text{daily account cap} )$$

---

## 2. Parameter Definitions

- **Base Reward Rates**:
  - Focus: 12 Focus Points per 50-minute completed sprint.
  - Avoided Emissions: 2.5 Impact Points per 1.0 kg CO2e reduced against a documented baseline.
  - Community Service: 25 Contribution Points per verified hour.
  - Offset Recognition: 100 points per 1.0 metric tonne CO2e retired on an authoritative registry.
- **Conversion Ratio**: 10 Points = 1.0 Base VTIME Credit.
- **Evidence Multipliers**:
  - `UNVERIFIED`: `0.0×`
  - `USER_ENTERED`: `0.2×`
  - `ESTIMATED`: `0.3×`
  - `RECEIPT_BACKED`: `0.8×`
  - `METERED` / `ATTESTED` / `REGISTRY_VERIFIED`: `1.0×`
- **Daily Caps**:
  - Focus Daily Cap: 50 VTIME
  - Impact Daily Cap: 50 VTIME
  - Contribution Daily Cap: 50 VTIME
  - Offset Daily Cap: 100 VTIME
  - Global Account Daily Cap: 200 VTIME

---

## 3. Utility Credit Boundary
VTIME is an internal, closed-loop ecosystem utility credit. It is **not cash, not an investment contract, not an offset, and not transferable** unless enabled under a future compliant program.
