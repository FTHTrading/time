# Aggregated Home Energy Efficiency Project MRV

## 1. Project Overview
- **Project Class**: Aggregated Home Energy Retrofit & Efficiency Program
- **Monitoring Period**: Quarterly (90 days)
- **Target Registries**: Gold Standard / Verra VCS
- **Role of ACNC Engine**: Ingests smart meter records, generates conservative reduction packages, and produces evidence bundles for independent validation.
