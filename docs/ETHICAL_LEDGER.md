# Ethical Ledger Boundary

## Core Tenet: Human Existence is Sovereign Value, Not an Environmental Liability

Traditional carbon calculators frequently err by treating ordinary human biological functions as an ecological debt. **ALL COUCH NO CAGE** formally rejects biological liability accounting.

---

## 1. What is Never Charged or Scored
The platform explicitly excludes and will never penalize:
1. **Respiration & Oxygen Intake**: Exhaled CO2 is part of the biogenic fast carbon cycle and is never calculated as an offset liability.
2. **Basal Metabolism & Energy**: Basal caloric requirements for staying alive are an inalienable human necessity.
3. **Bodily Existence & Sleep**: Sleeping, resting, or being present on Earth is never penalized.
4. **Essential Nourishment & Hydration**: Basic drinking water and nourishment needs are exempt from punitive scoring.
5. **Disability & Health Conditions**: Medical equipment, accessibility devices, and health-related requirements are never charged as excess emissions.

---

## 2. What We Model and Reward
The ledger focuses strictly on controllable, conscious choices and positive contributions:

- **Controllable Energy Consumption**: Home electricity tariffs, natural gas usage, smart thermostat efficiency.
- **Mobility Substitution**: Choosing public transit, cycling, walking, or remote work over solo fossil-fuel vehicle trips.
- **Material Stewardship**: Repairing devices over replacement, diverting organic waste, participating in certified reuse programs.
- **Digital Efficiency**: Optimizing AI compute and cloud storage workloads.
- **Positive Human Ledger**: Focused intellectual creation, mentorship, caregiving, community volunteer service, and environmental restoration.
