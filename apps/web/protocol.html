Non-Biological Ethical Ledger

Human existence is treated as sovereign positive value, never an ecological debt.

What We Measure & Reward

  • Focused deep work and skill acquisition
  • Caregiving, mentorship, and teaching
  • Community volunteer service
  • Physical hardware repair and reuse
  • Metered energy and transit reductions

What We Never Charge

  • Respiration or oxygen intake
  • Basal metabolism or caloric needs
  • Bodily existence and sleep
  • Basic water and food needs
  • Disability or medical device requirements

Reward Issuance Equation

Utility credits are calculated deterministically under published policy caps.

VTIME Issued = min( base_reward × evidence_multiplier × policy_multiplier, category_cap, daily_cap )
  • Daily Focus Cap: 50 VTIME
  • Daily Impact Reduction Cap: 50 VTIME
  • Daily Contribution Cap: 50 VTIME
  • Daily Account Maximum: 200 VTIME
