> [!CAUTION]
> **INVIOLABLE ETHICAL PRINCIPLE:** No human being may ever be charged, scored, penalized, taxed, metered, or negatively assessed for the biological, metabolic, or physiological processes required for life. This principle is algorithmically enforced and cannot be overridden by configuration, policy amendment, or administrative action.

## 1. Excluded Domains

The following categories are permanently excluded from all measurement, scoring, reward calculation, penalty assessment, or carbon quantification:

1. **Biological Human Functions:** Respiration, metabolic rate, oxygen consumption, sleep/rest, body temperature regulation.
2. **Health, Disability, and Medical Needs:** Medical conditions/treatments, disability-related equipment (wheelchairs, ventilators), basic nourishment.
3. **Basic Subsistence Needs:** Minimum nutritional needs, drinking water, basic non-discretionary shelter.
4. **Social Scoring & Behavioral Surveillance:** Subjective good-behavior surveys, lifestyle/habit surveillance, social media scoring.
