## 1. Key Identified Risks & Priority Mitigations

| Risk ID | Risk Area | Description | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **R-001** | **Methodology Fit** | Candidate methodology deemed inapplicable to aggregated residential heat pumps | Engage methodology advisor during pre-pilot diligence to obtain written opinion before significant investment. |
| **R-002** | **Additionality** | Interventions challenged under common-practice or investment barrier rules | Pre-validate additionality with regional census, ENERGY STAR market data, and utility program statistics. |
| **R-003** | **Data Integrity** | Interval/billing data incomplete or corrupted | Multi-source ingestion (API + bill upload) with strict 90% completeness threshold and automated flagging. |
| **R-004** | **Double Counting** | Overlapping utility DSM rebate or REC claim on the same premise | Multi-layer screening (utility records, Green Button, anti-double-counting affidavit, on-chain serial guard). |
| **R-005** | **Claims & Compliance** | Environmental claims perceived as overstating pilot progress | Strict enforcement of canonical product claim; FTC Green Guides review before any public communication. |
