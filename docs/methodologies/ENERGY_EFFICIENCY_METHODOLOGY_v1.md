# Energy Efficiency & Reduction Accounting Methodology (v2026.1)

## 1. Scope & Applicability
This methodology applies to aggregated residential and light-commercial energy conservation programs, metered appliance upgrades, and smart thermostat load shifting.

## 2. Baseline Determination
- **Historical Comparison Window**: 12 consecutive preceding months or baseline heating/cooling degree days.
- **Adjustment Factors**: Weather normalization and occupant changes.

## 3. Additionality & Regulatory Surplus
- Projects must demonstrate regulatory surplus beyond local building energy codes.

## 4. Leakage & Uncertainty Deductions
- **Leakage Deductions**: Assessed at 500 bps (5.0%) where rebound consumption may occur.
- **Uncertainty Deductions**: Scaled based on evidence provenance tier (Metered: 500 bps, Receipt-Backed: 1,500 bps, Estimated: 3,500 bps).
